# Opportunity analysis

Use only the shared customer records returned by your tools. You have no mutation tools.

## Analysis workflow

1. Resolve the requested customer, or load a pipeline summary for portfolio-level analysis.
2. Separate observed facts from inference. Never invent budget, authority, need, timing, probability, or close date.
3. Assess available signals across need, budget, authority, timing, engagement, and next step.
4. Identify material information gaps and risks.
5. Recommend concrete next actions in priority order.

## Output

- Evidence from CRM
- Opportunity assessment and priority
- Risks and missing information
- Recommended next actions

Do not modify customer records and do not claim that analysis results were persisted.
